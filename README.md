
# Carrefour Complete Master Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Carrefour Complete Master Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, transaction patterns, and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through 6 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and 11 charts, helping users explore different aspects of sales and customer data.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and transaction performance.
- Analyze average order value and items sold.
- Understand customer demographics and segment distribution.
- Explore sales trends across years, months, and weekdays.
- Compare performance across store locations and product categories.
- Evaluate customer ratings and discount rates.

## 📈 Dashboard Charts

The dashboard contains 11 visualizations:

### 1. Store Location Performance
Explores transaction amounts across different store locations to compare location-level performance.

### 2. Top Product Categories
Analyzes sales across product categories to help identify category-level performance.

### 3. Transaction Value Distribution
Examines transaction values to understand spending patterns and differences between purchases.

### 4. Statistical Spread (Box Plot)
Visualizes the distribution and spread of transaction values, helping identify variability and potential outliers.

### 5. Customer Gender Distribution
Shows how customers are distributed by gender.

### 6. Customer Age Groups
Analyzes customer spending across age groups, including Under 18, 18–30, 31–45, 46–60, and 60+.

### 7. Additional Segment Share
Displays the contribution of different customer segments to the overall transaction amount.

### 8. Yearly Sales Trend (2025–2026)
Compares total sales between 2025 and 2026 to highlight year-over-year performance.

### 9. Monthly Sales Performance
Analyzes sales across the 12 months of the year to explore monthly performance patterns.

### 10. Sales by Day of the Week
Examines sales across weekdays to understand how sales performance varies by day.

### 11. Average Customer Rating by Sub Category
Compares average customer ratings across product sub-categories to explore customer satisfaction.

## 🖱️ Interactivity

The dashboard uses Plotly.js to render interactive charts. Users can explore chart details through hover information and use the available chart controls for actions such as zooming and panning.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard brings together sales performance, customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and customer satisfaction in one view.

It provides a foundation for exploring business performance, understanding customer spending patterns, and identifying areas that may require further analysis.
